Terms of service

What we provide

FRB Contracting Services provides security systems installation, maintenance and monitoring provided by FRB Contracting Services Ltd, a private limited company registered in England and Wales under company number 15593792, certificated under [NSI OR SSAIB CERTIFICATION NUMBER]. IMPORTANT: police response to an intruder alarm is not automatic. Forces will issue a Unique Reference Number, and attend, only where the system has been installed and is maintained by a company certificated to the relevant standard by an approved inspectorate, and a URN may be withdrawn after repeated false activations; a system installed by a non-certificated company will not receive police response however it is advertised. CCTV recording identifiable people is the processing of personal data: the customer is the data controller and is responsible for completing a data protection impact assessment where required, displaying signage, setting and applying a retention period, responding to subject access requests, and registering with the Information Commissioner's Office where applicable, and the company will advise on system design to support those duties but cannot discharge them. Camera placement overlooking neighbouring property or public space engages further obligations under the UK GDPR and the Surveillance Camera Code of Practice. No security system prevents crime: systems deter, detect and record, and no guarantee is given that premises will not be burgled, damaged or entered. Services are described on the Services page. No physical goods are sold or shipped through this site.
